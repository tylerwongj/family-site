Speech Transcriber

Press a key, talk, press it again. Your words get typed for you.

  • Stays on your computer
  • Works offline after setup
  • Free, no account
This app is for computers. Send yourself the link and open it on your PC or Mac.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Open the file

    Find it in Downloads and double-click it.

  2. Blue warning? It's OK

    "Windows protected your PC" shows once. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Install, then wait once

    Click Install, Finish. A small Downloading model… pill shows in the corner the first time. Wait until it's gone.

Then: Right Alt Talk Right Alt Words appear

  1. Drag it to Applications

    Open the downloaded file, drag Speech Transcriber onto Applications, then open it from Applications.

  2. Mac warning? It's OK

    Shows once. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then Open.

  3. Allow 3 things

    The setup window walks you through each. Switch Speech Transcriber on:

    • Microphone to hear you
    • Input Monitoring for the key
    • Accessibility to type

    Still says Needs access? Click Restart Speech Transcriber.

Then: Right ⌘ Talk Right ⌘ Words appear

Updates

Mic icon by the clock Update available Yes

The app checks the new version is genuine before installing. Your settings, history and speech model stay.

No menu item? Click Check for updates… in the same menu.

Something off?

No "Run anyway" button

Click More info first. The button appears under it.

No "Open Anyway" on Mac

Try opening the app once, then look again in Privacy & Security within the hour.

Nothing appears after talking

Click into a text box first. If it says Copied, press Ctrl+V (Mac: ⌘V).

Lost what you said

Mic icon → Recent dictations, click it, paste.

Wrong microphone

Settings → Microphone.

The key does nothing

Mac: allow Input Monitoring (step 3). Any computer: mic icon → System Check.

Don't want it at startup

Settings → untick Launch quietly when I sign in.

Is it safe?

Private

Your voice never leaves your computer. No account, nothing uploaded.

Why the warning

Store apps pay for a digital "signature" Windows and Mac recognize. This free app has none, so your computer asks once.
